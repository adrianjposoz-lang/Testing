HOW TO PLAY

⚔️ COMBAT

Answer questions correctly to damage bosses. Wrong answers hurt YOU!

❤️ HEALTH

Your HP carries over between fights! Buy Health Potions from the shop to heal — you won't recover automatically.

🔥 COMBOS

3 correct in a row = COMBO (bonus gold). 5 in a row = CRITICAL HIT (double damage)!

⏱️ TIMER

Answer before time runs out or the boss attacks!

💡 HINTS

Use hints to eliminate a wrong answer. Die twice on a boss? Unlimited hints!

🛒 SHOP

Spend gold on gear and potions. Health Potions are essential — stock up before tough battles!

📖 CODEX

Each boss drops a Ledger fragment. Read them to master hard money lending!
